## 👨🏻‍💻 &nbsp;About Me

💡 &nbsp;I’m a backend and platform engineer with 9+ years of experience building systems that need to be **secure, scalable, and quietly reliable**. Most of my work lives behind APIs, auth layers, and platforms that other teams depend on.  

🧩 &nbsp;Over the years, I’ve moved from delivering individual backend features to owning **systems and platforms** used across teams—especially in **identity, access management, and payment-grade services**.  

🔐 &nbsp;I enjoy working on authentication, authorization, trust boundaries, and the kinds of problems where correctness and security matter more than flash.  

🎓 &nbsp;I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science and a Master’s in Software Development, with additional focus on AI/ML and Generative AI concepts.  

🌱 &nbsp;Currently curious about how identity systems evolve for **delegated and agent-driven workflows**, and how platforms can stay simple as complexity grows.

✉️ &nbsp;If you want to talk systems, platforms, or just say hi — feel free to reach out.
